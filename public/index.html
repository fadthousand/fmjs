About Us

CV. Family Makmur Jaya Sentosa is a company engaged in heavy equipment service, used forklift trading, and the provision of forklift spare parts. Located in Tangerang Regency, we are here to help fulfill the needs of maintenance, repair, and procurement of heavy equipment components for customers from various industrial sectors.

We understand that optimal heavy equipment conditions play an important role in supporting smooth operational activities. Therefore, we are committed to providing communicative services, helping customers identify technical needs, and offering solutions according to unit conditions.
